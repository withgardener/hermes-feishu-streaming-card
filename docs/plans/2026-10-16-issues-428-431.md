- Upstream has plugin declaration/discovery and transactional selection APIs (`pm.plugin_declarations`, `pm.plugins_state`, `pm.plugin_inputs`, `pm.client`). Determine the supported admission/registration entry point rather than editing PM facts or lockfiles.
